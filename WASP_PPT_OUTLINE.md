## Slide 2: The Core Challenge
*   **Fragmented Tools**: Swapping between Jira, Excel, text-differs, and email.
*   **Information Silos**: Critical code snippets scattered in private notes or chat history.
*   **Manual Reporting**: Daily time drain due to manual link compilation and case counting.
*   **Lack of Analytics**: No centralized view of team velocity or progress over time.

---

## Slide 3: Introducing WASP
*   **WASP** is the **Central Nervous System** for SE MAPPERS.
*   It combines:
    1.  **Code Governance** (Library)
    2.  **Utility Toolkit** (Formatter, Diff, CSV)
    3.  **Governance & Reporting** (Daily Status)
    4.  **Process Management** (Wiki & Maps)

---

## Slide 4: Feature Spotlight—The Code Library
*   **Problem**: "I've written this logic before, but I can't find it."
*   **Solution**: A high-performance, searchable library for the whole team.
*   **Benefit**: Faster onboarding for new members and higher code quality through reuse.

---

## Slide 5: Feature Spotlight—Developer Utilities
*   **Built-in Efficiency**:
    *   **Diff Checker**: Instantly compare map versions.
    *   **CSV Visualizer**: Turn raw logs into readable tables.
    *   **Formatter**: Ensure consistent standards across the team.
*   **Security**: Keeps data internal instead of pasting it into 3rd-party websites.

## Slide 6: Automated Daily Status (Reporting 2.0)
*   **Data Entry**: Quick, intuitive input with role-based management.
*   **Smart Counting**: Filters "On Leave/NA" to provide **True Velocity**.
*   **Reporting**: **Single-Click Email** to Leadership. Pre-filled, structured, and professional.
*   **Visual Proof**: Automated PNG snapshot of the day's successes.

## Slide 7: Team Intelligence—Wiki & Leaderboard

*   **Online Status**: Visibility on active team members for real-time collaboration.
